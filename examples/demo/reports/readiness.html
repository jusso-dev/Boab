Methodology

Boab inventories cryptography in source code, TLS endpoints and certificate stores, deduplicates the findings and scores each asset on five axes aligned to the ASD LATICE approach.

priority = round(algo_vuln × 0.30 + hndl × 0.30 + data_sens × 0.15 + sys_crit × 0.15 + mig_diff × 0.10, 1)

Tier 1 assets must be migrated inside the 2028 milestone window. Tier 2 fits inside the 2028 milestone but is harder to migrate. Tier 3 belongs in the 2029 to 2030 rollout window. Tier 4 needs monitoring only.

At-risk assets use algorithms a cryptographically relevant quantum computer is expected to break (RSA, ECDSA, ECDH, classic Diffie-Hellman, and broken legacy primitives), or have a status Boab could not determine. Data protected by them today can be harvested now and decrypted later.

Refer to the ASD Information Security Manual for control mappings. Boab does not map findings to specific ISM control IDs.
